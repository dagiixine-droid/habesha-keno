🎮 How to play Habesha Keno

Learn the basics in a few quick steps.

  1. 1
    Open Keno

    Tap Keno in the bottom navigation.

  2. 2
    Pick your numbers

    Select 1–10 numbers from the grid numbered 1 to 80, or use Random picks.

  3. 3
    Choose demo credits

    Set a virtual-credit amount between 10 and 500, then check your selection.

  4. 4
    Draw and compare

    Tap Draw numbers. The game draws 20 numbers and shows how many match yours.

Practice mode: credits have no cash value, and this prototype does not pay winnings or support real-money deposits or withdrawals.
