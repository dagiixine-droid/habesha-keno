🎮 How to play Keno X

Learn the basics in a few quick steps.

  1. 1
    Open Keno

    Tap Keno in the bottom navigation.

  2. 2
    Pick your numbers

    Select 1–5 numbers from the grid numbered 1 to 80, or use Random picks.

  3. 3
    Choose practice points

    Choose a virtual-credit stake between 10 and 500. Matching more of your numbers pays a bigger multiplier.

  4. 4
    Draw and compare

    Tap Draw numbers. The game draws 20 numbers and shows how many match yours.
